How to vote

Official sources · Rules differ by state, so always check your own

Voting in Missouri

  • Election Day: Tuesday, Nov 3 · polls open 6 a.m.–7 p.m. Anyone in line at 7 p.m. can vote.
  • Photo ID: required to vote in person, including early in-person absentee. Accepted IDs
  • Vote early, no excuse needed: in-person absentee voting runs Oct 20 – Nov 2 at your local election office. Hours vary by county.
  • Mail absentee: the request deadline (5 p.m. Oct 21) has passed. Ballots already requested must be received by 7 p.m. Nov 3.
  • Registration: closed Oct 7 for this election.

Before you watch

  • Early numbers move. States count mail, early and Election Day ballots at different times, so a lead with little of the vote in can shrink or flip.
  • Called means projected. A race shows as called here only once AP or a major network projects a winner.
  • The magic numbers. Senate: Republicans hold 53 seats, so Democrats need a net gain of 4 for 51 (the vice president breaks 50–50 ties). House: 218 seats for a majority.
  • Some races won’t finish tonight. Maine and Alaska may need ranked-choice counts, Georgia may go to a Dec 1 runoff, and Arizona, Nevada and California count for days.
